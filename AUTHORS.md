# Authors

## Cantay Caliskan, PhD

Cantay Caliskan is an Associate Professor of Instruction at the University of
Rochester's Goergen Institute for Data Science and Artificial Intelligence. His
research combines computer vision, generative AI, and network analysis to study
political polarization, political communication, and bias in artificial
intelligence. He holds a Ph.D. in Political Science from Boston University and
an MBA from Rochester's Simon Business School. Previously, he held faculty
appointments at Denison University and Ohio Wesleyan University. His work uses
large-scale visual, textual, and social media data to develop new approaches to
understanding political behavior.

## Mija Aleksandraviciute

Mija Aleksandraviciute is a University of Rochester undergraduate pursuing
degrees in Data Science and Economics, with graduation expected in spring 2027.
Her research combines artificial intelligence, econometrics, and computational
social science to examine historical worldviews, college student outcomes, and
entrepreneurship. She has held research positions at Rochester and NYU's Stern
School of Business and coauthored working papers on AI-based historical
worldview modeling and space-industry startups. Her professional experience
includes business intelligence, financial modeling, and sustainability
consulting. Her honors include an InnovateCS Grant and a first-place Best
Insight Award at ASA DataFest 2026. She also serves as Economics Chair of
Rochester's Finance & Economics Council and volunteers in college-access
advising.

For this project she collected the original game images and their metadata
(June–August 2026).
